# Updating your website

**Your site:** [aparnakallakuri.com](https://aparnakallakuri.com)

You don't need to write any code, install anything, or learn how any of this
works. You describe the change you want in plain English, Claude makes it, and
the website updates itself a minute later.

---

## The quickest way

1. Go to **[claude.ai/code](https://claude.ai/code)** and sign in.
2. Pick the **`aparnakallakuri`** project from your list.
3. Type what you want changed.
4. When Claude says it's done and pushed, wait about a minute and refresh your
   site.

That's the whole loop. Nothing to save, upload, or publish — pushing *is*
publishing.

---

## Asking for changes

Write the way you'd brief a colleague. You don't need to know what anything is
called or where it lives on the page.

Things you can just say:

> Change my headline to "Integrated marketing leader — strategy, content and
> communications across the GCC."

> I've left ZainTECH. Update my current role to say I'm open to senior marketing
> roles, and change the ZainTECH dates to end July 2026.

> Add a new job at the top of my career section: Head of Content at Acme, Dubai,
> from August 2026. Say I own the global content strategy.

> Add a testimonial from Priya Nair, Marketing Director at Zain — "Aparna turned
> our messaging around in a quarter."

> The About section is too long. Cut it to two paragraphs, keep the tone.

> Swap my photo for the new one — here it is. *(attach the image)*

> Add SEMrush and Ahrefs to my toolkit.

> Take the "$200M pipeline" number off — I'd rather lead with the 120%.

**A tip that makes a real difference:** tell Claude *why*, not just *what*.
"Make the intro punchier, I'm targeting CMOs not recruiters" gets you a better
result than "rewrite the intro."

## If you want to see it before it goes live

Just say so:

> Show me how this looks before you publish it.

Claude can make the change, show you, adjust it, and only publish once you're
happy.

To undo something after it's live:

> Undo the last change and put the site back how it was.

Nothing is ever lost — every version is kept, so anything can be reversed.

---

## Two things to know

**Your phone number is deliberately not on the site,** and it's been removed
from the CV people download. Personal numbers on public websites get harvested
by bots and sold. Email and LinkedIn are the ways people reach you. If you'd
rather have it on there, say so and it can be added — just know it will be
public.

**If you replace your CV,** give Claude the new file and say "update my CV."
Don't drop the file in yourself — the published copy has to be a specially
cleaned version with your number stripped out, and Claude knows how to make it.

---

## If something looks wrong

The site has automatic safety checks. If a change would break the page, it is
refused and **the version already live stays up untouched** — a broken edit can't
take your site down.

So if something looks off, it's almost always just a browser holding an old copy.
Try a hard refresh (**Cmd+Shift+R** on Mac, **Ctrl+Shift+R** on Windows).

Still wrong? Say:

> Something looks broken on the site — can you check and fix it?
