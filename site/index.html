Case studies

Four mandates in full — the context, the move that changed it, and the measured outcome.

ZainTECH · Positioning, GTM and integrated marketing

From company positioning to market execution

Context

As ZainTECH expanded its portfolio across cloud, AI, digital services, infrastructure and cybersecurity, the business needed a clearer way to articulate what it stood for — and to take that story consistently to market.

Move

Working with senior leadership, business leaders, sales and marketing, helped shape the Digital. Intelligent. Resilient. positioning, bringing a broad portfolio under one company narrative. Then made that positioning usable: GTM strategies and integrated programmes across priority offerings and markets, spanning campaigns, events, webinars, pitch decks, thought leadership, case studies, web, email and social, supported by an AI-powered content model.

Result

  • ~35% of company pipeline influenced by content, campaigns and events
  • 150+ demand marketing assets
  • 120% increase in website traffic and engagement

Soroco · Content strategy and category creation

Building a category, not just creating content

Context

Soroco was building its presence in an emerging category and needed a clearer market story. The brief covered content strategy across the business, but the larger challenge was shaping how the company and its category were understood.

Move

Worked across leadership, sales and marketing to connect market feedback with positioning and content strategy — repositioning the website and digital content, strengthening the CEO and CTO’s external presence, and building a consistent story across social and other channels. Analyst relations was a second route to market, engaging Everest Group, NelsonHall, Forrester and HFS Research.

Result

  • 100% improvement in web performance
  • 110% social engagement achieved
  • 4 major analyst firms engaged

IBM Consulting × MAFTECH · Change and internal communications

Turning “working Fridays” into “getting Sundays back”

Context

Assigned to help set up the internal communications function for 9,000+ employees. When the UAE moved to a Monday-to-Friday working week in January 2022, it was more than a change of working days: Friday carries deep cultural and religious significance in the GCC. An initial survey showed only 60–64% positive sentiment.

Move

Worked closely with senior management to shape the strategy and win their backing, particularly around respecting Friday prayer times — then shifted the conversation from “working Fridays” to “getting Sundays back”. The Reclaiming Sundays campaign invited employees to share what Sundays meant to them through photographs and stories about family, faith and friends. Their stories became the campaign.

Result

  • 9,000+ employees reached
  • +20 pts increase in positive sentiment
  • 80–85% positive towards the new working week

IBM WebSphere · Community, influencer and demand marketing

Reaching the people who influenced the purchase

Context

IBM WebSphere had around 34,000 users inside the 93,000-member IBM Middleware User Group. Enterprise purchases were signed off by senior decision-makers, but developers, architects and technical specialists had significant influence over what was actually adopted and used.

Move

Treated the community as somewhere to learn and participate rather than a channel to market to. Over three years, built a more active model using practitioner content, forums, challenges, peer recognition, product education and expert access — including a virtual “event-in-a-box” micro-event format combining expert sessions, smaller interactive discussions and direct access to IBM specialists, plus expanded participation from IBM Business Partners.

Result

  • $60M opportunities generated
  • $6M revenue through the virtual micro-event format
  • 2× community engagement over three years
  • 20+ additional Business Partners engaged
